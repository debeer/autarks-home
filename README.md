# Autarks' Home

A plug-and-play home appliance so people can own, run and govern their compute, data and publishing, rather than rent them from a distant cloud.

**Website:** [https://autarks-home.com](https://autarks-home.com)  
**Contact:** [john@debeer.com](mailto:john@debeer.com)

## What it is for

Autarks' Home is a plug-and-play, internet-enabled appliance that brings publishing and the management of information into the home, without requiring that its owner learn server administration. It serves three main purposes:

1. It enables disengagement from curated online services whenever the need for privacy and security outweighs the convenience of online services.
2. It establishes a platform by which owners may associate freely with peers who have similar or complementary needs.
3. As a planned option, it will bring affordable AI into the home, where it is controlled by the owner, while avoiding the expense of cloud APIs.

## What is open source, and what is not

The Autarks' Home appliance software will be open source under the GNU Affero General Public Licence (AGPL): defined as the software that sets up and runs the appliance and its planned AI peripheral, and the research applications, but excluding any and all add-on modules identified as commercial. You should not have to buy the appliance to use the software.

The guild management module and the data modeling module come later, as separate commercial products, and are not part of this open-source commitment. The open software does not depend on either.

The commitment is that if the company disappears, owners lose nothing: firmware and OS builds will be fully documented and published, and keys and backups stay in the owner's possession, so anyone can rebuild and maintain the system.

A `LICENSE` file will be added with the first code release. Until then, this repository contains design and planning documents only, and those documents do not grant a licence to any software.
